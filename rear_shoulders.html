💪 Rear Shoulders
Rear Delt Session
Posture & Health
🥊
Why rear delts matter: Striking athletes develop strong front delts but weak rear delts — this imbalance causes shoulder injuries. Strong rear delts keep your shoulders healthy, improve posture and balance the pulling muscles used in clinch and grappling.
💪 Exercise 1 — Reverse Flyes
Reverse Dumbbell Flyes
○
3 Sets
15 reps
30 sec rest
Light weight
Bent over position: Hinge at hips, arms hanging down with slight elbow bend
Raise out to sides: Lift arms out to the sides until parallel with floor — squeeze rear delts
Go light: Rear delts are small — 5-15lbs is plenty. Form beats weight here
Martial arts connection: Rear delt balance prevents the rotator cuff injuries that end fighters careers

💪 Exercise 2 — Band Pull-Aparts
Band Pull-Aparts
○
4 Sets
20 reps
20 sec rest
Arms at shoulder height: Pull band apart until it touches your chest — pinch shoulder blades
Slow negative: Control the return — don't let the band snap back
Do these every day: Pull-aparts are one exercise you can do daily — the rear delts recover fast and need the volume

💪 Exercise 3 — Face Pulls
Face Pulls (Band or Cable)
○
3 Sets
15 reps
30 sec rest
Pull to face: Pull band or cable to your face — elbows flaring out to sides
External rotation: Rotate hands back at the end — this is the key movement for shoulder health
Martial arts connection: This is non-negotiable for striking athletes — protects the rotator cuff from the wear caused by thousands of punches
