Leg Session B
Dumbbell
🥋
Weighted leg work for fighters: Adding resistance to lunges and single leg movements builds the unilateral strength needed for explosive single-leg takedowns. Romanian deadlifts build the hamstring strength that prevents the most common injury in martial arts — hamstring tears from high kicks and explosive movements.
🏋️ Set Your Dumbbell Weight
lbs
Lunges & Bulgarian: 15-40lbs each hand. RDLs: 25-60lbs each hand. Start lighter than you think.

💪 Exercise 1 — Weighted Lunges
Weighted Lunges
○
3 Sets
10 each leg
60 sec rest
Dumbbells at sides: Hold dumbbells at your sides — don't swing them as you lunge
Same form: Everything the same as bodyweight lunge — just heavier
Step length: Longer step = more glute and hamstring. Shorter step = more quad. Mix it up
Walking lunges: Walk forward instead of returning each rep — more dynamic and fight-specific
Martial arts connection: The weighted lunge builds the quad and glute strength for explosive wrestling shots and the leg drive needed in clinch battles

💪 Exercise 2 — Romanian Deadlift
Romanian Deadlift
○
3 Sets
10 reps
60 sec rest
▶️
Romanian Deadlift — Form Guide
Hip hinge mechanics explained clearly
Hip hinge: Push your hips BACK — not down. The bar stays close to your legs throughout
Soft knees: Slight bend in the knees — not a squat, not straight legs. Find the middle
Flat back: Your spine stays neutral throughout — no rounding, especially at the bottom
Feel the hamstrings: You should feel a strong stretch in the back of your legs — if not push hips back further
Martial arts connection: Hamstring strength and hip hinge power — the foundation of every takedown, throw and explosive hip movement in combat sports

💪 Exercise 3 — Bulgarian Split Squat
Bulgarian Split Squat
○
3 Sets each leg
8 reps
60 sec rest
Chair or bench needed
▶️
Bulgarian Split Squat — Form Guide
The hardest single leg exercise — watch before attempting
Rear foot elevated: Place rear foot on a chair or bench — front foot about 2 feet in front
Lower straight down: Drop your back knee toward the floor — don't let it drift forward past your toes
Front knee tracks toe: Keep front knee aligned with your foot — no caving inward
This will be humbling: Start lighter than you think — this exercise is brutally effective and hard
Martial arts connection: Single leg strength is the most fight-specific leg exercise — every stance, kick and takedown relies on single leg stability

💪 Exercise 4 — Weighted Calf Raises
Weighted Calf Raises
○
3 Sets
15 reps
20 sec rest
Hold dumbbells: Hold at your sides or on your shoulders — more resistance means more calf growth
Step edge: Stand on a step with heels hanging off — full range of motion going below parallel
Pause at top: Hold the peak contraction for 1 second — squeeze the calves hard
Single leg option: One dumbbell, one leg — twice as hard, twice as effective
Martial arts connection: Calf and ankle strength is the foundation of all footwork — the explosive push-off for every strike, step and level change
