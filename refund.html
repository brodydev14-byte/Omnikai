Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026
Before launch: replace the [BRACKETED] placeholders and decide the question in the blue box below, then delete both boxes. Not legal advice.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from your profile page — open Profile → Manage, which takes you to Stripe's billing portal, and cancel there. No email, no asking us, no retention hoops.

When you cancel, you keep Warrior access until the end of the month you have already paid for. Nothing is taken away early. After that date your account drops back to the free Wanderer tier — you do not lose your account, your history, your streak or your personal records.

Refunds

Because cancelling already leaves you with the time you paid for, we do not normally refund part-used months.

We will refund you in full if:

  • You were charged after cancelling.
  • You were charged twice for the same month.
  • Omnikai was unavailable for a long stretch of the month you paid for.
  • You subscribed by accident and have not used the paid content — tell us within 48 hours.
  • We closed your account without good reason.
Decide before launch: do you want a no-questions money-back window — say 7 or 14 days — on a first subscription? It costs little at your size, removes the main reason someone hesitates at the checkout, and is far cheaper than a chargeback. If you want one, say so here explicitly. If you would rather not, delete this box and the policy above stands as written.

Your legal rights come first

If you are in the UK, EU or another place with a statutory cooling-off period for digital purchases, those rights apply whatever this page says, and we will honour them.

How to ask

Email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your account and say what happened. We will reply within [2 BUSINESS DAYS]. Approved refunds go back to the original card through Stripe and usually appear within 5–10 business days.

If you think something has gone wrong with a charge, please email us before contacting your bank. A chargeback costs us a fee and takes weeks; we can usually just fix it the same day.
