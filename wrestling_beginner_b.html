🤼 Drill 2 — Hip Heist
Hip Heist
○
3 Sets
8 each side

▶️
Hip Heist — Wrestling Scramble Movement
The key to winning scrambles
From all fours or knees: Post one hand, swing your hips through to the opposite side — a full rotation of your hips
Continuous flow: Heist one way, then immediately heist the other — develop rhythm and explosiveness
Why it matters: The hip heist is how you reverse position in a scramble — it's what separates wrestlers who win scrambles from those who get pinned in them

Granby Roll
○
3 Sets
5 each side

Soft surface recommended
▶️
Granby Roll — Turtle Escape
Essential for escaping bottom position
From turtle position: On hands and knees, roll diagonally over one shoulder to escape and face your opponent
Shoulder not neck: Roll over the point of the shoulder — never the head or neck
End facing up: You should end up on your back or in a seated position facing where your opponent would be
Why it matters: The granby is the primary escape from turtle position in wrestling — crucial for not getting pinned or taken to your back
