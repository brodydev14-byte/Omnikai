💪 Exercise 1 — Seated Lateral Raises
Seated Lateral Raises
○
2 Sets
8-12 reps

▶️
Seated Lateral Raise — Perfect Form
The most common shoulder exercise done wrong
Seated: Sit on the edge of a chair or bench — removes momentum and isolates the shoulders
Slight bend: Small bend in the elbow throughout — never lock out straight
Lead with elbows: Think of leading the movement with your elbows not your hands
Stop at shoulder height: Going higher shifts the work to your traps — stop at parallel to the floor
Slow down: 2 seconds up, 2 seconds down — no swinging
Martial arts connection: Side deltoid strength keeps your guard arms up in late rounds when fatigue sets in

💪 Exercise 2 — Front Raises
Dumbbell Front Raises
○
2 Sets
10 reps
30 sec rest
Alternate arms
Alternate arms: Raise one arm at a time — keeps tension constant and improves balance
Raise to eye level: Stop at eye level — not overhead
Thumbs up: Rotate so thumbs point up at the top — activates the front delt fully
No leaning back: Keep your torso upright — no swinging or using momentum
Martial arts connection: Front delt strength powers the jab and straight — the most thrown punches in combat sports

💪 Exercise 3 — Arnold Press
Arnold Press
○
2 Sets
8 reps
45 sec rest
▶️
Arnold Press — Form Guide
The rotation is what makes this special
Start position: Dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing YOU (like end of a curl)
The rotation: As you press up, rotate your palms to face AWAY — this rotation hits all three heads of the deltoid
Full press: Press all the way up, arms nearly straight at the top
Slow and controlled: The rotation should be smooth — don't rush it
Martial arts connection: The rotation pattern mirrors the shoulder rotation in uppercuts and hooks — training the exact movement path
