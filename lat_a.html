💪 Exercise 2 — Bodyweight Rows
Bodyweight Rows
○
3 Sets
10 reps
45 sec rest
Table or low bar
▶️
Bodyweight Rows — Setup & Form
How to set up at home with a table
Setup: Lie under a sturdy table, grab the edge with overhand grip, body straight like a plank
Pull chest to table: Squeeze shoulder blades together as you pull up — lead with your chest not your arms
Body stays straight: Don't let your hips sag — maintain that plank position throughout
Harder version: Elevate your feet on a chair — more bodyweight = harder pull
Easier version: Bend your knees more — less bodyweight engaged
Martial arts connection: The pulling motion directly trains the clinch pulling and guard pull strength used in BJJ and wrestling

💪 Exercise 3 — Band Pulldowns
Resistance Band Pulldowns
○
3 Sets
12 reps
30 sec rest
Resistance band needed
Setup: Anchor band overhead in a door or wrap around a pull up bar — kneel or sit below
Pull to chest: Pull the band down to your upper chest — elbows drive down and back
Squeeze lats: At the bottom squeeze your lats hard — imagine trying to put your elbows in your back pockets
No band? Skip this exercise and add an extra set of bodyweight rows instead
Martial arts connection: The pulldown motion is exactly what you do when pulling someone into a clinch or dragging them to the ground

💪 Exercise 4 — Pull Up Progression
Pull Up Progression
○
3 Sets
Pick your level below
60 sec rest
📊 Find Your Level
0 reps
Band assisted pull ups or jumping pull ups — use a resistance band looped over the bar for assistance
1-3 reps
Negative pull ups — jump to top position, lower yourself as SLOWLY as possible (5-10 seconds down). Do 3-5 negatives per set
4-9 reps
Full pull ups — do as many as you can with good form, rest, repeat. Track your max each session
10+ reps
You're ready for Session C — weighted pull ups and variations await you! 🔥
Dead hang start: Always start from a full dead hang — no half reps from a bent arm position
Pull elbows down: Think of pulling your elbows to your hips — not pulling your hands to your chest
Chin over bar: Full rep = chin clears the bar — no partial reps
Martial arts connection: Pull ups are arguably the single best exercise for combat athletes — every grappling movement uses these exact muscles
