Boxing Stance & Weight Transfer
Understanding your power base — watch first
60% weight on back foot: This loads your power. Your rear foot is a spring — push off it when you punch
Hip rotation drill: Without punching, practice rotating your hips forward and back — feel the torque
Knee bend: Stay low — power comes from pushing up through the legs, not standing tall
Shoulder position: Lead shoulder slightly raised — it protects your chin AND adds reach

🏋️ Dumbbell Variation Active
Use 2-5lb dumbbells. Slow deliberate jabs — feel the shoulder engagement. This builds the exact muscles used in power punching. Keep weight under 5lbs — form is everything.
▶️
Power Jab Technique
Focus on the hip drive and weight transfer
Slow it down: This is NOT about speed. Throw each jab at 60% speed and feel every muscle engage
Drive from the ground: Push off your back foot, rotate hip, THEN extend the arm — in that order
Full extension: Extend completely — power lives at the end of the punch, not the middle
Guard stays up: Right hand NEVER drops — even on slow power jabs

The Power 1-2 Combination
Hip rotation and weight transfer for maximum impact
Load the cross: As you throw the jab, your weight shifts back slightly — this LOADS the rear hand for maximum power
Squish the bug: Pivot your rear foot as you throw the cross — imagine squishing a bug under your heel
Exhale hard: Sharp exhale on BOTH punches — this engages your core and adds 10-15% more power
No rushing: 1… pause… 2. Feel each punch land with full commitment before the next

💪 Pushup Max Test
Do as many pushups as you can with PERFECT form. Chest to floor, full extension, core tight. No half reps — quality beats quantity. This sets your baseline and tomorrow's goal.
