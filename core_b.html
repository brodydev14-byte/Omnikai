Why rotational power matters: Every punch, kick, throw and submission involves rotation. The stronger your rotational core, the more power you generate from your hips and the harder you are to sweep or off-balance.

⭐ Exercise 1 — Russian Twist
Russian Twist
○
3 Sets
Go to near failure
30 sec rest
▶️
Russian Twist — Proper Form
Watch the feet and spine position carefully
Legs up: Slightly lift both legs off the floor and keep them together throughout — this is what makes it a real core exercise
Rotation: Rotate left and right, touching the floor on each side with your hands — that's one rep
Lean back: Keep your torso at about 45 degrees — not sitting straight up, not lying flat
Go to near failure: Push through the burn — stop just before your form breaks down
The higher your legs: The harder it gets — start low if you're new, raise them as you get stronger
Martial arts connection: This is the exact rotational movement used in hooks, body shots and throwing in BJJ/Judo

💪 Exercise 2 — Leg Raises
Floor Leg Raises
○
3 Sets
10 reps
30 sec rest
▶️
Leg Raises — Form & Variations
Lower back position is critical — watch first
Setup: Lie flat on your back, hands under your glutes or by your sides, legs straight
Lower back: Press your lower back into the floor — never let it arch up during the movement
Lower slowly: The hardest part is lowering slowly — resist gravity on the way down
Don't touch the floor: Stop just before your feet hit the ground — keep the tension on your core
Martial arts connection: Hip flexor and lower ab strength — critical for guard retention in BJJ and high kicks in striking arts

💪 Exercise 3 — Bicycle Crunches
Bicycle Crunches
○
3 Sets
16 reps total
30 sec rest
Slow and controlled
Movement: Hands behind head, bring opposite elbow to opposite knee while extending the other leg
Don't pull your neck: Hands behind head for support only — your core does the work, not your arms
Slow it down: 2 seconds per rotation — slow bicycle crunches are 10x harder and more effective than fast ones
Full rotation: Really rotate your torso — don't just move your elbows
Martial arts connection: The rotational pattern mirrors the hip rotation in hooks and body shots

💪 Exercise 4 — Side Plank
Side Plank Hold
○
2 Sets each side
20 sec each
20 sec rest
Setup: On your side, elbow directly under shoulder, feet stacked, hips lifted
Keep hips up: Don't let them sag toward the floor — squeeze your obliques the whole time
Easier version: Drop your bottom knee to the floor — still effective, builds up to full side plank
Martial arts connection: Oblique and lateral core strength — crucial for clinch work, sprawling and ground fighting
