💪 Glutes · Dumbbell
Glute Session B
Dumbbell
🥋
Weighted glute work for fighters: Adding load to hip thrusts and single leg movements develops the explosive power that separates recreational fighters from serious athletes. Single leg RDLs fix the imbalances that cause injuries in kicking and explosive movements.
🏋️ Set Your Dumbbell Weight
lbs
Hip thrusts: 25-60lbs across hips. Sumo squats: hold one heavy dumbbell. Single leg RDL: 20-40lbs each hand.

💪 Exercise 1 — Dumbbell Hip Thrusts
Dumbbell Hip Thrusts
○
3 Sets
12 reps
60 sec rest
Dumbbell placement: Rest dumbbell across your hips — hold it in place with both hands
Same form as bodyweight: Upper back on bench, drive through heels, squeeze at top
Progress over time: When 12 reps feels easy, add weight. Track it session to session
Martial arts connection: Loaded hip extension builds the explosive power needed for takedown completions and hip escapes under pressure

💪 Exercise 2 — Sumo Squats
Dumbbell Sumo Squats
○
3 Sets
12 reps
45 sec rest
Wide stance: Feet wider than shoulder-width, toes pointed out 45 degrees
Hold one dumbbell: Hold one heavy dumbbell vertically between your legs — or one in each hand
Sit between your legs: Push knees out and sit straight down — feel the inner thigh and glute stretch
Drive through heels: Push hips forward as you rise — squeeze glutes hard at the top
Martial arts connection: Wide stance strength mirrors the base position in wrestling and BJJ — the sumo squat directly trains your ability to maintain a strong, low base

💪 Exercise 3 — Single Leg RDL
Single Leg Romanian Deadlift
○
3 Sets each leg
8 reps
45 sec rest
▶️
Single Leg RDL — Form Guide
Balance and hip hinge explained
Stand on one leg: Hold dumbbell in opposite hand to standing leg — cross-body pattern
Hinge at hip: Push hip back as you lower the dumbbell toward the floor — back stays flat
Rear leg extends: Let your rear leg extend back behind you as a counterbalance — body forms a T shape
Balance is the point: Start without weight if needed — the balance challenge is building proprioception
Martial arts connection: Single leg balance and hamstring strength — every kick, every stance transition, every explosive movement relies on single leg stability

💪 Exercise 4 — Lateral Lunges
Dumbbell Lateral Lunges
○
3 Sets
10 each leg
45 sec rest
Step wide to the side: Take a large lateral step — sit into that hip while the other leg stays straight
Bent knee tracks toe: Knee of the bending leg tracks over the foot — don't cave inward
Chest up: Keep torso upright — don't fold forward over the bent leg
Push back to center: Drive through the heel of the bent leg to return to standing
Martial arts connection: Lateral movement strength — the exact hip and glute pattern used in side steps, sprawls and lateral takedown defense
