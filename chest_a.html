📊 Day 1 Max Test

Tomorrow's target: — reps 💪
Hand position: Slightly wider than shoulder-width, fingers pointing forward
Elbows at 45°: Don't let elbows flare straight out — 45 degree angle protects the shoulder
Chest to floor: Full range of motion — chest should nearly touch the ground each rep
Core tight: Don't let your hips sag — plank position the whole time
Martial arts connection: Pushup strength directly translates to punch power, clinch control and ground fighting

💪 Exercise 2 — Wide Pushups
Wide Grip Pushups
○
3 Sets
10 reps
30 sec rest
Targets outer chest
Hands wider: Place hands significantly wider than shoulder-width — this shifts focus to the outer chest
Elbows flare more: On wide pushups elbows flare out more — this is correct for this variation
Slower movement: Go slower than standard pushups — feel the stretch at the bottom
Martial arts connection: Outer chest width helps with clinch control and creating frames against opponents

💪 Exercise 3 — Diamond Pushups
Diamond Pushups
○
3 Sets
8 reps
30 sec rest
Inner chest + triceps
Hand position: Form a diamond shape with thumbs and index fingers touching under your chest
Elbows back: Elbows track back toward your body — not flaring out
Harder than standard: This is significantly harder — reduce reps if needed
Martial arts connection: Inner chest and tricep strength — the punch extension power at the end of a straight

💪 Exercise 4 — Decline Pushups
Decline Pushups
○
2 Sets
8 reps
30 sec rest
Upper chest focus
Feet elevated: Place feet on a chair, couch or step — the higher your feet the more upper chest activation
Same form: Keep same body alignment as standard pushup — just angled
Start low: If new to decline — use a low surface first, maybe 12 inches off the ground
Martial arts connection: Upper chest strength helps with underhooks, over-under clinch battles and pushing opponents away
