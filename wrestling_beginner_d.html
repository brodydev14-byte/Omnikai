🤼 Drill 1 — Stance & Level Changes
Stance & Level Changes
○
3 Sets
10 reps
20 sec rest
Get low in your wrestling stance then drive back up — stay in athletic position the whole time, never stand all the way up
Add footwork: Between level changes, circle left and right — stay moving like you would in a real match

🤼 Drill 2 — Penetration Steps
Penetration Steps
○
3 Sets
8 each side
30 sec rest
Level change then drive: Drop level, lead knee drives to the mat between imaginary opponent's feet, back knee comes up, drive through with hips
This is the most important move in wrestling: Every takedown starts here
Rep Counter (Each Side)
0
🤼 Drill 3 — Duck Unders
Duck Unders
○
3 Sets
8 each side
30 sec rest
Drop and spin: Drop your level and spin under an imaginary opponent's arm — ending up behind them
Head outside: Your head goes outside their hip as you spin through
End in control position: You should end up behind where your opponent would be — ready for a rear body lock
Why it matters: Duck unders create back takes — one of the most dominant positions in wrestling and MMA
Rep Counter (Each Side)
0
🤼 Drill 4 — Sprawl & Recover
Sprawl & Recover
○
3 Sets
10 reps
20 sec rest
Standing start: From wrestling stance, explosively kick both legs back while dropping your hips — then snap back to stance immediately
Speed of recovery matters: Getting back to your feet fast after the sprawl is just as important as the sprawl itself
This builds the reflex: Sprawling on a shot has to be automatic — drilling it alone builds the pattern
