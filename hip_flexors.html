💪 Hip Flexors
Hip Flexor Session
Mobility & Strength
🦵
Why hip flexors matter: Hip flexors lift your leg for every kick and knee strike. Tight hip flexors reduce kicking height, speed and power. Strong flexible hip flexors are what separates fighters who throw effortless high kicks from those who struggle to get their leg above the waist.
💪 Exercise 1 — Leg Raises
Lying Leg Raises
○
3 Sets
12 reps
30 sec rest
Lower back flat: Press your lower back into the floor throughout — if it arches you've gone too low
Slow negative: Lower legs as slowly as possible — the eccentric phase is where hip flexor strength is built
Don't touch floor: Stop just before feet touch — keep the tension on your hip flexors
Martial arts connection: Direct hip flexor strengthening — the muscle that lifts your leg in every kick, knee and takedown defense
Rep Counter
0
💪 Exercise 2 — Dynamic Leg Swings
Dynamic Leg Swings
○
3 Sets
20 each leg
20 sec rest
Front to back: Swing leg forward and back in a controlled arc — gradually increasing range
Side to side: Then swing leg across your body and out to the side — 20 each direction
Hold something: Hold a wall or post for balance — focus on the swing not the balance
Martial arts connection: Dynamic hip mobility — increases kicking range of motion and warms up the hip for explosive movements
Rep Counter (Each Leg)
0
💪 Exercise 3 — Hip Flexor Lunge Stretch
Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch
○
3 Sets
60 sec each side
15 sec rest
Kneeling lunge position: One knee on floor, other foot forward — sink hips forward and down
Squeeze glute: Squeeze the glute of the kneeling leg — this increases the stretch on the hip flexor
Tall posture: Don't lean forward — stay upright to maximize the hip flexor stretch
Martial arts connection: Tight hip flexors are why fighters can't throw high kicks — this directly addresses that limitation
Hold Timer — Each Side
1:00
💪 Exercise 4 — Mountain Climbers
Mountain Climbers
○
3 Sets
30 sec each
20 sec rest
High plank position: Drive knees alternately toward chest as fast as possible
Hips stay down: Don't let your hips pike up — keep the plank position throughout
Martial arts connection: Dynamic hip flexor strength AND cardio — simulates the rapid leg pumping used in wrestling and BJJ scrambles
Timer
0:30
