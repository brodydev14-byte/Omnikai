Perfect Plank Form
Most people do this wrong — watch first
Neutral spine: Don't let your hips sag OR pike up — your body should be a straight line from head to heels
Squeeze everything: Glutes tight, quads tight, core braced — the plank works your whole body not just abs
Breathe: Don't hold your breath — slow controlled breathing while holding tension
Martial arts connection: This is the same bracing you use when taking a body shot or resisting a takedown

▶️
Dead Bug — Perfect Form
The most underrated core exercise for athletes
Setup: Lie on your back, arms straight up, knees bent at 90 degrees in the air
Movement: Lower opposite arm and leg toward the floor simultaneously — slow and controlled
Key: Your lower back must stay pressed into the floor the ENTIRE time — if it lifts you've gone too far
Exhale as you lower: Breathing out helps brace the core and keep your back flat
Martial arts connection: Anti-rotation strength — same pattern used when grappling and resisting sweeps

▶️
Hollow Body Hold Tutorial
A gymnastic core staple — harder than it looks!
Position: Lie on back, arms overhead, legs straight and lifted — lower back pressed into floor
The hollow: Think of pulling your belly button to your spine — creating a "hollow" shape
Too hard? Bend your knees or keep arms by your sides — build up gradually
Martial arts connection: The hollow position is your core stability base for every movement in combat sports

💪 Exercise 4 — Bird Dog
Bird Dog
○
3 Sets
10 reps each side
30 sec rest
Setup: On all fours, wrists under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat like a table
Movement: Extend opposite arm and leg simultaneously, hold 2 seconds, return slowly
Don't rotate: Your hips should stay completely square to the floor — no twisting
Slow is better: 3 seconds out, 2 second hold, 3 seconds back — control beats speed here
Martial arts connection: Lower back and anti-rotation stability — critical for BJJ ground work and wrestling
