Boxing Stance & Footwork Basics
Watch before drilling — 4 min tutorial
Stance: Feet shoulder-width, lead foot forward, knees bent, hands up, chin down
Step and slide: Move lead foot first in your direction — never cross your feet
Circle: Circle left for 30 sec, circle right for 30 sec — feel the balance
In and out: Step in toward imaginary opponent, step back out — practice your range

How to Throw a Perfect Jab
Watch before your first set
Visualize: Picture a real opponent in front of you — shadowboxing works best with full mental focus
Hip rotation: Rotate your lead hip slightly as you throw — this is your power source
Snap back: Retract the jab as fast as you throw it — speed both ways
Guard stays up: Right hand never drops — not even for a second
