Muay Thai Stance & Footwork Basics
Different from boxing — wider, more upright
Muay Thai stance is upright: More upright than boxing — your torso is straighter to allow hip rotation for kicks
Wider stance: Feet slightly wider than shoulder-width — this gives you a stable base for kicking
The bounce: Muay Thai has a distinctive rhythmic bounce — light on your feet, weight shifting side to side
Lead foot angle: Lead foot points forward or slightly out — rear foot at 45 degrees
