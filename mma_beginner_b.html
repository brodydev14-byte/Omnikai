💪 Drill 1 — Power Stance & Heavy Hands
Rooted Stance Power Strikes
○
1-2
Jab — Power Cross
3 Sets
1:30 each

Power starts at the floor: Drive off the back foot, rotate the hip, then the shoulder — the arm moves last, not first
Slower than Session A: Half the volume, twice the intent — every cross is thrown to end the fight
Stay balanced: If a power shot leaves you falling forward you are wide open for the takedown — recover to stance every time

🤼 Drill 2 — Explosive Double Leg
Power Level Change & Drive
○
2-TD
Power cross — Explosive shot
4 Sets
6 each side
45 sec rest
Level change with the legs: Bend the knees and keep the chest up — bending at the waist hands your opponent your neck
Explode through, not to: The takedown is a sprint into the hips, not a reach — the back leg keeps driving the whole way
Reset fully between reps: This is a power drill — if the shot gets slower you are training conditioning instead. Take the rest

🛡️ Drill 3 — Sprawl to Counter
Heavy Sprawl & Power Counter
○
SP-2-3
Sprawl — Cross — Hook off the scramble
4 Sets
8 reps
45 sec rest
Sprawl heavy: Hips slam down, legs kick back hard — your bodyweight is the weapon that stops the shot
Pop straight back up: Hands up, back to stance, then two power shots — the counter after a stuffed takedown is one of the highest percentage moments in MMA
Power under fatigue: Your fourth set will feel heavy — that is exactly when you learn to keep the strikes hard

💪 Strength — Explosive Pushups
Clap or Fast Pushups
○
3 Sets
10 reps
60 sec rest
Push off the floor: Explode hard enough that the hands leave the ground — clap if you can, fast reps if you cannot yet
Why it matters: The same explosive push drives your cross and posts you back up out of bottom position
Stop the set when speed drops: Slow reps build size, fast reps build punching power
