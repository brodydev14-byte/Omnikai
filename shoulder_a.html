Why shoulders matter in martial arts: Shoulder endurance is what separates fighters who can throw crisp punches in round 5 from those who drop their hands by round 2. Strong stable shoulders also protect you from injury when posting, clinching and grappling.

💪 Exercise 1 — Pike Pushups
Pike Pushups
○
3 Sets
8 reps
30 sec rest
▶️
Pike Pushup — Form & Variations
The best bodyweight shoulder press — watch first
Setup: Start in a downward dog position — hips high, arms straight, forming an inverted V
Lower head toward floor: Bend elbows and lower the top of your head toward the ground between your hands
Elbows out: Let elbows flare slightly — this is a shoulder movement not a chest movement
Too easy? Elevate your feet on a chair — harder the higher your feet go
Martial arts connection: Directly builds the overhead pressing strength used in clinch work and takedown defense

💪 Exercise 2 — Arm Circle Burnout
Arm Circle Burnout
○
3 Sets
45 sec each
20 sec rest
Arms straight out
Arms straight out: Hold arms parallel to the floor — no dropping them even when it burns
Small circles first: Start small, gradually get bigger — feel the burn build up
Switch direction: Forward for half the time, backward for the other half
This will burn: That's the point — this is shoulder endurance training. Fighters NEED this
Martial arts connection: Simulates keeping your guard up for extended rounds — the exact endurance needed in striking arts

💪 Exercise 3 — Plank Shoulder Taps
Plank Shoulder Taps
○
3 Sets
16 taps total
30 sec rest
Setup: High plank position — wrists under shoulders, body straight, core tight
Tap: Lift one hand and tap the opposite shoulder — alternate sides
Don't rotate: Keep your hips square — the challenge is resisting the rotation as you lift each hand
Feet wider = easier: Wider stance makes it more stable — narrow feet is the harder version
Martial arts connection: Shoulder stability and anti-rotation — directly translates to ground fighting and posting on the mat

💪 Exercise 4 — Lateral Raises (Improvised)
Improvised Lateral Raises
○
2 Sets
10 reps
30 sec rest
Use what you have: Water bottles, canned food, a backpack with books — anything with slight resistance works
Arms slightly bent: Small bend in the elbow — don't lock out straight
Raise to shoulder height: No higher — going above shoulder level takes the work off the delts
Slow and controlled: 2 seconds up, 2 seconds down — feel the side delts working
No weight? Do it anyway — even air resistance with slow controlled movement builds endurance

💪 Exercise 5 — Wall Handstand Hold
Wall Handstand Hold
○
2 Sets
15 sec each
30 sec rest
Optional — skip if beginner
Face the wall: Kick up facing the wall — safer than back to wall for beginners
Hands shoulder width: Place hands about 6 inches from the wall
Push through shoulders: Don't collapse — actively push the floor away to keep shoulders engaged
Skip if not ready: This is advanced — do an extra set of pike pushups instead if you're not comfortable
