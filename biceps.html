💪 Biceps
Biceps Session
Grip & Pull
🥋
Why biceps matter in martial arts: Bicep and grip strength are essential for clinch work, submission holds, guard pulls and any pulling movement in grappling. Strong biceps help you maintain grips longer and escape holds faster.
💪 Exercise 1 — Dumbbell Curls
Dumbbell Curls
○
3 Sets
10 reps
45 sec rest
Supinate at top: Rotate wrist so palm faces ceiling at the top — maximizes bicep contraction
No swinging: Keep elbows pinned to your sides — no body momentum
Slow negative: 3 seconds lowering — this is where strength is built
Martial arts connection: Bicep strength for clinch pulls, guard retention and submission holds

💪 Exercise 2 — Hammer Curls
Hammer Curls
○
3 Sets
10 reps
45 sec rest
Neutral grip: Thumbs pointing up throughout — hits the brachialis and forearm too
Alternate arms: Curl one at a time for better focus and control
Martial arts connection: Brachialis and forearm strength — the grip muscles used constantly in grappling
Rep Counter
0
💪 Exercise 3 — Dead Hang Grip Work
Dead Hang Grip Work
○
3 Sets
30 sec each
30 sec rest
Dead hang: Hang from a bar with full bodyweight — builds grip endurance like nothing else
Vary grip: Overhand, underhand, one hand — each variation hits differently
Martial arts connection: Grip strength is the most important attribute for grapplers — you can't submit someone you can't hold
