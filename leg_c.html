⭐ Exercise 1 — Barbell Squat
Barbell Back Squat
○
2 Sets
6-8 reps
2 min rest
Safety bars or spotter
▶️
Barbell Back Squat — Complete Guide
Bar position, depth and safety
Bar position: High bar (on traps) for more quad. Low bar (on rear delts) for more posterior chain. Try both
Brace hard: Big breath in, brace your core like you're about to get hit before every rep
Knees out: Drive knees out over toes throughout — never let them cave inward under load
Depth: Hip crease below parallel — anything less is a partial rep
Always use safeties: Set the safety bars before every heavy set — no exceptions
Martial arts connection: The squat is the king of all strength exercises for combat athletes — raw leg power translates to every single movement in fighting

💪 Exercise 2 — Leg Press
Leg Press
○
2 Sets
10-12 reps
90 sec rest
Leg Press Machine
Foot position: High and wide hits more glutes and hamstrings. Low and narrow hits more quads
Don't lock out: Stop just short of full extension — keeps tension on the muscles and protects the knees
Full depth: Lower until your knees are at 90 degrees — don't short the range of motion
Martial arts connection: Leg press lets you load more volume than squats — builds the quad mass and endurance needed for long training sessions and fights

💪 Exercise 3 — Leg Extensions
Leg Extensions
○
2 Sets
12 reps
45 sec rest
Leg Extension Machine
Slow and controlled: 2 seconds up, 2 seconds down — no swinging or using momentum
Full extension: Extend completely at the top — hold for 1 second to fully contract the quad
Toes pointed up: Dorsiflexed foot (toes toward you) activates the quads more fully
Martial arts connection: Quad isolation — the muscle that extends your knee in every kick, every step and every explosive movement

💪 Exercise 4 — Leg Curls
Lying Leg Curls
○
3 Sets
10 reps
45 sec rest
Leg Curl Machine
Hips down: Keep your hips pressed into the pad throughout — don't lift them to swing the weight
Full range: Curl all the way up to full contraction — feel the hamstring squeeze hard
Slow negative: 3-4 seconds lowering — this is where hamstring strength is built
Martial arts connection: Hamstring strength is the most important injury prevention for fighters — and powers every explosive hip movement, kick and sprawl

💪 Exercise 5 — Weighted Calf Raises
Barbell or Machine Calf Raises
○
3 Sets
15 reps
20 sec rest
Barbell on back: Use the squat rack — barbell on your traps, stand on a plate for full range
Or calf raise machine: Same principles — full range of motion, slow negative
Pause at bottom: Let heel stretch below platform before each rep — full stretch maximizes growth
High reps work great: Calves respond well to high rep sets — don't be afraid to do 20-25 reps
Martial arts connection: Heavy loaded calf work builds the ankle and foot strength for explosive footwork — the base of all striking power
