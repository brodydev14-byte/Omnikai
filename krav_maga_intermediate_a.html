Simultaneous Defense and Attack
○
4 Sets
2 min
The core Krav Maga principle: when attacked, deflect the threat and counter-attack simultaneously — not sequentially
Incoming right hook: redirect the arm outside with your left hand while your right palm strikes the face — one motion
This removes the delay between defense and offense that gets people hurt in real situations

Weapon Defense Drilling
○
3 Sets
8 each side
Knife defense: redirect the weapon hand outside, control the wrist, counter-strike to the face or throat
This is for gross motor skill development — fine motor skills fail under adrenaline, gross motor skills persist
Krav Maga knife defense is about controlling the weapon hand immediately — not waiting for an opening

Multiple Attacker Awareness
○
3 Sets
2 min
Move constantly: never let one attacker fix your position while another flanks you
Strike and move: every counter-strike is followed by movement to a new angle
This is the hardest Krav Maga scenario to train solo — but the movement patterns can be drilled
