⭐ Exercise 1 — Barbell Hip Thrusts
Barbell Hip Thrusts
○
3 Sets
10 reps
90 sec rest
▶️
Barbell Hip Thrust — Complete Guide
Setup, padding and progressive overload
Use a pad: Place a barbell pad or folded mat under the bar — directly on hip bone is painful
Bar placement: Bar sits in the hip crease — not on your stomach or thighs
Full extension: Drive hips up until body is perfectly straight from knees to shoulders — squeeze HARD
Chin tucked: Look straight ahead or slightly down — not up at the ceiling
Progressive overload: Add weight every session when possible — this exercise responds very well to progressive overload
Martial arts connection: Loaded barbell hip thrusts build more glute strength than any other exercise — the direct power source for every explosive combat sport movement

💪 Exercise 2 — Sumo Deadlift
Sumo Deadlift
○
2 Sets
6-8 reps
2 min rest
▶️
Sumo Deadlift — Form Guide
Wide stance mechanics and hip engagement
Wide stance: Feet wider than shoulder-width, toes angled out 45 degrees — hands inside your legs
Knees out: Push knees out toward your toes as you pull — opens the hips and activates glutes more
Hips closer to bar: Sumo stance puts your hips closer — more vertical torso than conventional
Drive hips through: Lock out by driving hips forward — squeeze glutes hard at the top
Martial arts connection: The sumo deadlift directly mimics the wide base used in wrestling — and builds the hip and glute strength to maintain and drive out of that position

💪 Exercise 3 — Cable Kickbacks
Cable Kickbacks
○
3 Sets each leg
15 reps
30 sec rest
Cable Machine
Ankle attachment: Attach cable to ankle — stand facing the machine, slight lean forward
Kick straight back: Extend leg straight back — squeeze glute hard at full extension
Don't swing: Controlled movement — no momentum. Glute must do all the work
Squeeze and hold: 1 second hold at full extension before returning
Martial arts connection: The kickback motion directly trains the glute max extension used in kicks — especially the rear kick and side kick

💪 Exercise 4 — Abductor Machine
Hip Abductor Machine
○
3 Sets
15 reps
30 sec rest
Abductor Machine
Full range: Push legs apart as far as the machine allows — feel the outer glute and hip working
Slow negative: Control the return — don't let the weight slam back
Sit upright: Good posture — leaning forward changes the muscle being worked
Martial arts connection: Hip abductor strength is critical for kicking power, lateral footwork and maintaining a wide stable base in grappling
