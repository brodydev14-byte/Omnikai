○

▶️
Dumbbell Shoulder Press — Form Guide
Seated vs standing, elbow position explained
Seated is safer: Seated press removes lower back strain — better for fighters who already stress their spine
Elbows at 90°: Start with dumbbells at ear level, elbows bent at 90 degrees
Press straight up: Drive the dumbbells straight overhead — slight arc is fine
Don't lock out: Stop just short of full lockout — keeps tension on the shoulders
Martial arts connection: Overhead pressing strength translates directly to clinch control and pushing opponents in grappling

💪 Exercise 2 — Lateral Raises
Lateral Raises
○
2 Sets
8-12 reps
45 sec rest
Go lighter: Most people go too heavy on laterals — use a weight where you can control the full range
Slight forward lean: Lean very slightly forward — this puts the side delt in better mechanical position
Pinky up at top: At the top of the movement, slightly rotate so pinky is higher than thumb — full delt activation
Slow negative: 3 seconds lowering — the negative is where real growth happens

Cable Machine
▶️
Cable Face Pulls — Why Every Fighter Needs This
Shoulder health and rear delt balance
Cable at face height: Set the cable at about face level, use a rope attachment
Pull to face: Pull the rope toward your face, elbows flaring out to the sides
External rotation at end: At the end of the pull, rotate your hands back — this is the key movement
Light weight, high reps: This is a health exercise — don't go heavy, go controlled
Martial arts connection: Rear delt and rotator cuff health — prevents the shoulder injuries that end fighters careers. Non-negotiable!

💪 Exercise 4 — Upright Rows
Dumbbell Upright Rows
○
2 Sets
10 reps
45 sec rest
Wide grip: Hold dumbbells shoulder-width — narrow grip puts more stress on the shoulder joint
Lead with elbows: Pull elbows up and out — dumbbells follow, don't lead
Stop at chest height: Don't pull past your chest — going higher stresses the shoulder impingement zone
Martial arts connection: Traps and side delts together — builds the thick neck and trap musculature that protects fighters from knockdowns
