💪 Exercise 1 — Bodyweight Squats
Bodyweight Squats
○
Max Test then 3 Sets
20 reps target

▶️
Perfect Squat Form
Depth, knee tracking and back position
📊 Day 1 Max Test

Tomorrow's target: — reps 💪
Feet shoulder width: Toes turned out 15-30 degrees — find what feels natural for your hips
Knees track toes: Don't let knees cave inward — push them out over your pinky toes
Depth: Hips below parallel if you can — deeper squat = more muscle activation
Chest up: Keep your torso upright — don't fold forward excessively
Martial arts connection: The squat is the foundation of every takedown, level change and stance in combat sports

💪 Exercise 2 — Jump Squats
Jump Squats
○
3 Sets
10 reps
60 sec rest
Explosive power
Load and explode: Squat down to parallel then explode upward as fast as possible — land softly
Soft landing: Land with bent knees — absorb the impact, don't slam down
Arms help: Swing your arms up as you jump — adds height and mimics real athletic movement
Rest between sets: These are explosive — full rest between sets to maintain power output
Martial arts connection: Explosive hip extension is the exact power source for takedowns, kicks and sprawls — this builds it directly

💪 Exercise 3 — Lunges
Bodyweight Lunges
○
3 Sets
10 each leg
45 sec rest
Step forward: Take a large step forward — back knee drops toward the floor
90 degree angles: Both knees at 90 degrees at the bottom — front knee stays over ankle
Upright torso: Keep your chest up — don't lean forward excessively
Push back with front heel: Drive through your front heel to return to standing
Martial arts connection: The lunge pattern is identical to the penetration step in wrestling takedowns — you're literally training your shot

💪 Exercise 4 — Nordic Curls
Nordic Hamstring Curls
○
3 Sets
5 reps
60 sec rest
Hardest bodyweight leg exercise
▶️
Nordic Curl — Form & Progressions
Start with assisted version if needed
Setup: Kneel on a soft surface, anchor your feet under something heavy or have a partner hold them
Lower slowly: Control your descent toward the floor as slowly as possible — this is the hardest part
Catch yourself: Use your hands to catch yourself when you can't hold anymore — push back up
Too hard? Do the lowering phase only (eccentric) — lower as slow as you can and use arms to push up
Injury prevention: Nordic curls reduce hamstring injury risk by up to 50% — critical for kickers and wrestlers
Martial arts connection: Hamstring strength and injury prevention — essential for high kicks, sprawls and explosive level changes

💪 Exercise 5 — Calf Raises
Bodyweight Calf Raises
○
3 Sets
20 reps
20 sec rest
Full range: Rise all the way up on your toes then lower heel below the step if possible
Slow negative: 3 seconds lowering — the stretch at the bottom is where growth happens
Single leg variation: One leg at a time doubles the intensity
Martial arts connection: Calf strength powers the push-off in footwork, the spring in your stance and the ankle stability for all kicking arts

💪 Exercise 6 — Fighting Stance Hold
Fighting Stance Hold
○
3 Sets
45 sec each
20 sec rest
Fight specific endurance
Get in stance: Take your boxing or fighting stance — feet staggered, knees bent, weight on balls of feet
Stay low: Maintain the bend in your knees throughout — don't stand up when it burns
Stay active: Bounce slightly — don't just stand still, stay in fighting rhythm
Why this matters: Most fighters gas out in their legs before their cardio fails — this directly trains fight stance endurance
