Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The short version

We collect what the app needs to work and nothing else. There are no analytics, no tracking pixels and no advertising anywhere in Omnikai — not Google Analytics, not Meta, not anything. We do not sell your data and we have nothing to sell it for.

Who is responsible

Brody Harris, Tennessee, United States. Questions, requests or complaints: brodydev14@gmail.com.

What we store

WhatWhy
Email addressTo identify your account and let you sign in or reset your password
PasswordStored only as a secure hash by our auth provider. We never see it
Your display nameTo greet you in the app
Chosen martial arts, equipment, gym accessTo pick which sessions to show you
Body stats you enter (weight, height, goal)To estimate calorie and macro targets
Workout history, streak, personal records, achievementsProgress tracking
Food log (most recent 60 days)So your nutrition log follows you between devices
Membership tier and subscription statusTo know whether to unlock paid content

Your theme preference stays on the device and is never sent anywhere. Food logs older than 60 days remain only on the device that recorded them — we don't collect them.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. Nothing else — no contacts, no Drive, no other Google data.

What we do NOT store

  • Your card details. Payment happens on Stripe's own pages. Card numbers never touch our servers. We keep only Stripe's customer and subscription identifiers, your subscription status and renewal date.
  • Your location, contacts, photos, or anything from your device beyond what you type in.
  • Any behavioural or advertising profile.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles sign-in. Your account and training data live there, on servers in the US (us-east-2).
  • Stripe — processes payments and holds your billing details under its own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the website itself.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr — serve the fonts and one JavaScript library. Loading a page means your browser contacts them, so they see your IP address. We send them nothing about you.
  • YouTube — some sessions link to tutorial videos. Nothing is embedded, so YouTube learns nothing unless you click through.

If you are outside the US, note that your data is processed in the US.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. Delete your account and your profile and training data are deleted with it. Stripe keeps payment records for its own legal and tax obligations, which we do not control.

Your rights

Email brodydev14@gmail.com and we will, within 30 days:

  • Send you a copy of everything we hold about you
  • Correct anything wrong
  • Delete your account and data

Depending on where you live (for example the UK, EU or California) you may have further rights, including objecting to processing and complaining to a data protection regulator. We will not make you jump through hoops to use any of them.

Cookies

We set no advertising or analytics cookies. We use browser storage for one purpose: keeping you signed in and holding your own training data on your device. Clearing your browser data signs you out.

Security

Your data is protected at the database level, not just in the app: the database itself enforces that you can only read and write your own records. Paid content is withheld by the database rather than hidden by the page. Passwords are hashed, and connections use HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever discover a breach affecting your data, we will tell you.

Children

Omnikai is not intended for under-16s. If you believe a child has created an account, contact brodydev14@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will tell you rather than quietly updating the date.
