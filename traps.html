💪 Traps & Neck
Traps Session
Neck & Upper Back
🥊
Why traps matter in martial arts: Thick traps and a strong neck protect you from knockdowns and reduce concussion risk. Trap strength also powers the shrug motion used in clinch work, underhooks and defending takedowns.
💪 Exercise 1 — Dumbbell Shrugs
Dumbbell Shrugs
○
3 Sets
15 reps
30 sec rest
Straight up: Shrug straight up toward your ears — don't roll shoulders forward or back
Hold at top: 1-2 second hold at the top — squeeze the traps hard
Heavy is fine: Traps are strong muscles — don't be afraid to use significant weight
Martial arts connection: Trap thickness protects your neck in clinch battles and when taking strikes

0
💪 Exercise 2 — Neck Bridges
Neck Bridges
○
3 Sets
30 sec each
20 sec rest
Front bridge: On back, bridge up so top of head touches floor — hold. Start gentle
Start slowly: Neck training requires gradual progression — don't rush this
Wrestlers use this: Neck bridges are a staple of wrestling training for a reason — a strong neck is armor
Martial arts connection: Neck strength reduces concussion risk and improves head position in clinch and ground fighting
Seconds Held
0
💪 Exercise 3 — Band Pull-Aparts
Band Pull-Aparts
○
3 Sets
20 reps
20 sec rest
Arms at shoulder height: Hold band in front with both hands, pull apart until band touches chest
Squeeze at end: Pinch shoulder blades together at the end of each rep
Martial arts connection: Upper trap and rear delt health — essential for shoulder injury prevention in striking athletes
