💪 Triceps
Triceps Session
Push Power
🥊
Why triceps matter in martial arts: The tricep is the primary muscle responsible for punch extension — the final snap of every jab, cross and push. Strong triceps also help in pushing opponents away, posting on the ground and maintaining frames in BJJ.
💪 Exercise 1 — Tricep Dips
Tricep Dips
○
3 Sets
12 reps
45 sec rest
Chair or bench
Stay upright: Keep torso straight — don't lean forward or it becomes a chest exercise
Elbows back: Elbows track straight back — not flaring out to the sides
Full range: Lower until upper arms are parallel to the floor — no partial reps
Martial arts connection: Tricep extension strength is the final snap in every punch — this directly builds punching power

0
💪 Exercise 2 — Diamond Pushups
Diamond Pushups
○
3 Sets
10 reps
30 sec rest
Diamond hand position: Thumbs and index fingers touching under your chest
Elbows track back: Don't flare elbows — keep them close to your body
Martial arts connection: Inner chest and tricep combination — punch extension and pushing strength
Rep Counter
0
💪 Exercise 3 — Overhead Tricep Extension
Overhead Tricep Extension
○
3 Sets
12 reps
45 sec rest
One dumbbell
Hold one dumbbell overhead: Both hands cup the top plate — arms straight above your head
Only elbows move: Lower the dumbbell behind your head by bending elbows only — upper arms stay still
Full stretch: Get a good stretch at the bottom before extending back up
Martial arts connection: Long head tricep — builds the snap and extension power in uppercuts and overhands
Rep Counter
0
