💪 Lats · Dumbbell
Lat Session B
Dumbbell
🥋
Dumbbell lat work for fighters: Single arm rows fix the muscle imbalances that fighters develop from always leading with one side. Pullovers stretch the lats through a full range that mirrors the overhead reaching used in throws and takedowns.
🏋️ Set Your Dumbbell Weight
lbs
Rows: 25-60lbs typically. Pullovers: lighter, 15-35lbs. Use what lets you feel the lat working.

▶️
Single Arm Dumbbell Row — Form
The most important back exercise for fighters
Brace on bench: Place one knee and hand on bench — flat back like a table, no rounding
Pull to hip: Row the dumbbell toward your hip — not your armpit. This maximizes lat engagement
Elbow back: Drive your elbow back and up — think of starting a lawnmower
Full stretch at bottom: Let the dumbbell hang fully at the bottom — get that lat stretch before each rep
Martial arts connection: The single arm row is the exact motion of pulling an opponent off balance in wrestling and Judo — you're training your takedown setup

💪 Exercise 2 — Bent Over Rows
Bent Over Dumbbell Rows
○
3 Sets
10 reps
45 sec rest
Both arms
Hip hinge: Hinge at the hips until your torso is nearly parallel to the floor — back flat, not rounded
Row to lower chest: Pull both dumbbells toward your lower ribs — squeeze shoulder blades together at the top
Control the negative: Lower slowly — 2-3 seconds down each rep
Martial arts connection: Double underhook strength — the most dominant position in wrestling. This is how you train it with weights

💪 Exercise 3 — Dumbbell Pullovers
Dumbbell Pullovers
○
2 Sets
10 reps
45 sec rest
Go lighter
▶️
Dumbbell Pullover — Lat Focus
How to keep tension on the lat not the tricep
Setup: Lie across a bench, only upper back on bench — hips slightly lower, feet flat
Hold the dumbbell: Both hands cupping the top plate — arms slightly bent throughout
Arc overhead: Lower the dumbbell in an arc behind your head — feel the massive lat stretch
Pull back with lats: Think of pulling your elbows toward your hips as you bring it back — not pushing with arms
Martial arts connection: The overhead stretch and pull mirrors throws in Judo and the lat strength needed for takedown sprawls
