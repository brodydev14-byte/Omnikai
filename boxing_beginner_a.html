Boxing Footwork for Beginners
Watch before drilling — 4 min tutorial
Stance: Feet shoulder-width apart, lead foot forward, knees slightly bent, weight on balls of feet
Step and slide: Always move the foot closest to your direction first — never cross your feet
Pivot: Rotate on your lead foot to change angles — this is how you avoid and attack at the same time
Stay bouncy: Light rhythmic movement keeps you ready to move in any direction instantly

How to Throw a Perfect Jab
FightCamp — watch before your first set
Hip rotation: Slightly rotate your lead hip as you throw — this is where your power comes from, not your arm
Guard up: Your right hand stays glued to your chin at all times while jabbing
Snap it back: The jab is about speed — punch and retract fast, don't leave it extended
Step with it: Slight step forward with the jab — don't just arm punch from a static position

Jab & Cross — The 1-2 Combination
FightCamp — perfect your 1-2
Reset between combos: Return to your stance after every combination — don't just throw and freeze
Exhale on each punch: Short sharp breath out with every strike — tightens your core too
Eyes forward: Look at your imaginary target, not at your hands
Rhythm: 1… 2… reset. Find the rhythm and stick to it. Speed comes later
