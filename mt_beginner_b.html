Muay Thai Power Kick Mechanics
Hip drive and follow-through for maximum force
Throw your whole body: Your entire body weight goes into this — you should almost spin past the target
Follow through: Don't stop at the target — kick THROUGH it. Imagine your shin continuing past the bag
Step at 45 degrees: Before kicking, step your lead foot out at 45 degrees — this opens your hip for more rotation
Exhale sharply: Explosive exhale as you kick — condenses your core and adds significant power

Muay Thai Knee Strikes — Form Guide
The most powerful close range weapon
Drive your hip through: The knee strike is all hip drive — thrust your hip forward as you bring the knee up
Point your toes down: Flex your foot down — this engages the hip flexor more fully for the upward drive
Clinch setup: In real Muay Thai knees come from the clinch — imagine pulling opponent's head DOWN as your knee comes UP
Rising knee vs straight: Rising knee (upward) targets the body and chin. Straight knee (forward) targets the midsection
