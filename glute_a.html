Glute Session A
No Equipment
🥋
Why glutes are your engine: The glutes are the largest and most powerful muscle in your body. Every kick, takedown, throw and punch rotation originates from glute power. Weak glutes = weak everything. Strong glutes = unstoppable force.
🔥 Warm Up
Glute Activation
○
3 Minutes
Hip circles — 10 each direction, loosen the hip joint
Glute squeeze — lie on back, squeeze glutes hard for 5 sec, release, 10 reps
10 slow bodyweight bridges — just activating before the workout
💪 Exercise 1 — Glute Bridges
Glute Bridges
○
3 Sets
20 reps
30 sec rest
Setup: Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat on floor hip-width apart
Drive through heels: Push through your heels to lift — not your toes
Squeeze at top: Hold the top position for 2 seconds, squeezing glutes as hard as possible
Full extension: Hips fully extended at the top — body forms a straight line from knees to shoulders
Martial arts connection: The hip extension pattern is identical to the hip drive in every takedown, kick and punch — this is fundamental movement training

💪 Exercise 2 — Hip Thrusts
Bodyweight Hip Thrusts
○
3 Sets
15 reps
30 sec rest
Bench or couch needed
▶️
Hip Thrust — Setup & Form
Upper back position and foot placement explained
Upper back on bench: Shoulder blades rest on the edge of a bench or couch — not your neck
Feet position: Feet flat, hip-width, close enough that shins are vertical at the top
Drive through heels: Same as bridges — push through heels, not toes
Chin tucked: Keep chin tucked to chest — don't hyperextend your neck
Hip thrusts vs bridges: Hip thrusts have greater range of motion — more glute activation than bridges
Martial arts connection: The hip thrust is literally the same motion as a wrestling shot completion and hip escape in BJJ

💪 Exercise 3 — Donkey Kicks
Donkey Kicks
○
3 Sets
15 each leg
20 sec rest
On all fours: Hands under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat
Kick back and up: Keep knee bent at 90 degrees, kick heel toward ceiling — squeeze glute hard at top
Hips stay square: Don't rotate your hips — keep them parallel to the floor throughout
Controlled movement: No swinging — slow and controlled hits the glute much better
Martial arts connection: Glute medius strength — important for hip stability in single leg stances and kicking arts
Rep Counter (Each Leg)
0
💪 Exercise 4 — Fire Hydrants
Fire Hydrants
○
3 Sets
15 each leg
20 sec rest
Same starting position: On all fours, back flat
Lift leg out to the side: Keep knee bent at 90 degrees, raise leg out to the side like a dog at a fire hydrant
Hip height: Raise until your thigh is parallel to the floor — don't go higher or you lose glute engagement
Squeeze at top: Hold for 1 second at the top before lowering
Martial arts connection: Hip abductor strength — critical for lateral movement in footwork and kicking power from the hip
Rep Counter (Each Leg)
0
💪 Exercise 5 — Lateral Band Walks
Lateral Band Walks
○
3 Sets
15 steps each way
20 sec rest
Resistance band around knees
Band position: Loop resistance band just above or below your knees
Athletic stance: Slight squat position — knees bent, hips back, chest up
Step sideways: Step out with one foot, follow with the other — keep tension in the band throughout
No band? Skip this and add an extra set of fire hydrants
Martial arts connection: Lateral glute strength for lateral footwork — the stepping motion used in boxing and the hip strength for takedown defense
