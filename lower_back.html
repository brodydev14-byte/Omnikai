💪 Lower Back
Lower Back Session
Spine Health
🥋
Why lower back matters: The lower back is the link between your upper and lower body. Every takedown, throw, hip escape and strike transfers force through this area. A strong lower back prevents the most common long-term injury in martial arts.
💪 Exercise 1 — Superman Holds
Superman Holds
○
3 Sets
12 reps
30 sec rest
Lie face down: Arms extended overhead, legs straight — lift everything off the floor simultaneously
Hold 2 seconds: Squeeze glutes and lower back at the top — hold before lowering
Slow and controlled: No bouncing — smooth controlled reps protect the spine
Martial arts connection: Lower back extension strength — essential for posture in all fighting stances and preventing the hunched-over fatigue position

💪 Exercise 2 — Bird Dogs
Bird Dogs
○
3 Sets
10 each side
30 sec rest
On all fours: Wrists under shoulders, knees under hips, back flat like a table
Opposite arm and leg: Extend right arm and left leg simultaneously — hold 2 seconds
Don't rotate: Hips stay perfectly square to the floor — this is the whole challenge
Martial arts connection: Anti-rotation lower back stability — critical for BJJ ground control and wrestling scrambles
Rep Counter (Each Side)
0
💪 Exercise 3 — Back Extensions
Back Extensions
○
3 Sets
15 reps
30 sec rest
Bench or floor
On a bench: Lie face down on bench with hips at the edge — lower upper body down then raise back to parallel
On the floor: Lie face down, hands behind head, lift your chest off the floor — same movement, less range
Don't hyperextend: Stop when your body is straight — going further stresses the spine
Martial arts connection: Erector spinae strength — maintains upright posture in late rounds when fatigue causes fighters to hunch forward and expose themselves
